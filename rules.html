📜 Rules

The Rules of the People's Republic of Floptropica are the foundational laws that govern our glorious nation. All citizens must abide by these rules.

Official Rules

  1. All Flops must change their name to "flop #" with the number according to the order in which Flops joined the Order
  2. All Flops must receive every role which doesn't give permissions
  3. All Flops should recruit at least one new Flop (optional)
  4. Members of the moderation or staff team are allowed to add a suffix to denote rank (nobody else can)
  5. No AI of any sort
  6. No slander of the Flops
